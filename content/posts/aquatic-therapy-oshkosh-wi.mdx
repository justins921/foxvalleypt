## Why water?

When you step into a therapeutic pool, something remarkable happens: the water supports as much as **80–90% of your body weight**. That buoyancy takes the load off your joints, your spine, and your healing tissues. Movements that feel impossible on land — walking, squatting, even just standing comfortably — suddenly become possible again.

At the same time, water provides gentle, even resistance in every direction. That means you're building strength with every motion, without the jarring impact of a treadmill or a gym floor. And because our pool is kept warm, your muscles relax, your circulation improves, and the whole experience simply feels good.

## Who benefits most from aquatic therapy?

Aquatic therapy is one of the most versatile tools we have. It's especially effective for:

- **Post-surgical rehabilitation** — knee, hip, shoulder, and spine recoveries
- **Arthritis and joint pain**, where land-based exercise is often too painful to start
- **Chronic pain and fibromyalgia**
- **Balance disorders**, where the water reduces the fear and risk of falling
- **Neurological conditions**
- **Athletes** recovering from impact injuries who need to keep moving safely

If you've struggled to get started with traditional therapy because it simply hurts too much, the pool may be the answer.
