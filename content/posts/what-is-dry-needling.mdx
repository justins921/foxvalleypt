If you've been fighting a stubborn knot in your neck, shoulder, or back that just won't release, dry needling may be the tool that finally brings relief. At Fox Valley Physical Therapy in Oshkosh, our certified specialists use it every day to help patients move and feel better.

## How dry needling works

Dry needling uses thin, sterile needles inserted directly into **trigger points** — the tight, irritable bands of muscle that cause pain and restrict your movement. When the needle reaches the trigger point, it helps the muscle release, calms the surrounding pain signals, and restores healthy blood flow to the area.

Many patients feel noticeable relief and improved range of motion within just a few sessions.

## Isn't that just acupuncture?

It's a fair question, but no. Although both use thin needles, **dry needling is not acupuncture**. Acupuncture is rooted in traditional Chinese medicine and the flow of energy along meridians. Dry needling is a modern, Western technique based on anatomy, neuroscience, and the latest research on how muscles and pain work.
